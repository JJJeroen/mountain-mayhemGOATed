# Brutal review, 2026-10-12

> **Update 2026-10-13.** Status of the items below: #1 Ram: real but smaller than guessed (ramCrossOnly fixed it; 153 -> 132 turns, goat win rates unchanged). #3 quick track is now the default. #4 and #7 fixed together by Summit Pledges (see RULES.md v0.3): hedging gave a +3.7 hay edge under the old rule and none under the new one. #5 fixed. #6 hay floor kept deliberately and documented. #8 tiebreak decided. #10 Headbutt wording fixed to match the engine. #2 and #9 remain a playtest question.

Method: read HANDOFF, RULES, engine.js, bots.js; ran `node test.js` (8 pass); wrote probes for suspected bugs; played full games through the new web UI (headless, 3 viewports). Three independent reviewer-agent runs stalled and returned nothing, so this is a single-reviewer pass: treat it as one opinion.

Confidence key: [verified] = ran it / read the exact code, [inferred] = reasoning from the rules and sim numbers, [guess].

## Ranked problems

1. **The Ram makes the finish line unreachable for the first mover each stage. [verified]** `engine.js moveGoat` checks the Ram before the finish. A goat on space 20 that rolls 2 with the Ram unused goes to *last place*, it does not finish (probe: `finished=false, ram=true`). From stage 2 on, every goat that is already past 17 is "first to end a move on or past 17" the moment it moves, so the leader is sent to last whenever it happens to be the first herd card. The race can only end after someone has been heartbroken that stage. It is funny once and infuriating by stage 5. Fix: Ram triggers only for a goat that *crosses* 17 from below (`from < ram <= dest`), not for goats already beyond. Not applied: this changes the balance numbers and needs a re-sim.
2. **Midpoint leader wins 22% (chance is 20%). [inferred from the handoff's own sim numbers]** Positions barely predict the winner, so the first half of a race is cosmetic and 12-point final bets are coin flips until late. Combined with #1 this is the real fun risk. Fix candidates: apply #1, shorten the track (15), or make the Ram a *one-off per race* rather than per stage. Re-sim after each.
3. **Pacing: ~150 turns, 8 stages. [from the handoff's sim]** With humans at 10-15 seconds a turn that is 25-35 minutes for a "party game", and 150 turns is ~38 each. The 15-space "quick" variant (~98 turns) should be the default for human play. Small to change (`cfg.finish/mud/ram`), but not balanced in the sim for 3 players or for the UI.
4. **IP: the economy is a near 1:1 re-skin of Camel Up. [inferred]** Leg-bet tokens with a descending stack, 2nd place pays 1, wrong costs 1, a "roll" token worth +1, face-down overall winner/loser bets with descending payouts and -1 for wrong, stacking racers that carry each other. Mechanics are not protectable, but this is the same *shape*, and store/trade-dress risk is about the whole package. Herd deck, tricks, Ram and mud are real differentiators, but I'd change the money structure (e.g. different token ladder, no "second place pays 1") before any commercial launch, and get a trademark/store search plus an IP lawyer. Not legal advice.
5. **Per-token hay clamping made scoring order-dependent. [verified, FIXED]** At 0 hay, legs `[+6, -1]` gave 5 one way and 6 the other. `scoreStage` now sums the stage and clamps once (both `engine.js` copies; tests pass). Impact on balance numbers: negligible.
6. **Hay floor at 0 gives broke players free downside. [inferred]** A player at 0 can place wild final/leg bets at no risk. Mild comeback mechanic, but it also makes the endgame lurch. Decide deliberately (allow negative hay, or keep and document).
7. **Final-bet hedging is cheap. [inferred]** Betting "Winner" on all five goats guarantees exactly one correct bet at a cost of 4 hay plus 5 turns of not running (5 hoof). Payout depends on placement order among correct bets, so whoever finishes placing first profits most. The bots never try it; humans will. Check with a bot that hedges.
8. **No end-of-game tiebreak. [verified, undefined in RULES.md]** The UI shows a shared win. Pick one (most correct final bets, then most hoofs, or share).
9. **Sim blind spots. [inferred]** Four copies of the same bot play each other (self-play equilibrium), "smart beats random 98.7%" says little about human skill, Run is hard-coded to value 1.0, bots ignore what opponents' scouting reveals, and hay inflation (mean ~40) hides that most of it is positive-sum hoofs and legs. Real fun still needs human playtests.
10. **Rules-text ambiguity. [verified]** Headbutt in RULES.md ("the goat above the bottom goat of your choice swaps with the one below") does not read like the engine ("any goat riding on another swaps with the one beneath"). The UI uses the engine behaviour and labels it plainly.

## Smaller notes
- Last card of a stage is public information (only one goat left), so betting on it is nearly free money for whoever acts first; this is inherent and fine, but keep an eye on it.
- Bots receive the full state object including deck order and hands; fine for local play, a hard blocker for online (server-side engine, per-seat views).
- "3 players" ambiguity: the start screen now offers 2 or 3 opponents (3 or 4 seats); 3 seats has not been re-balanced.

## Verdict
Safe to build the page on the current engine (done). Before playtests: apply #1 (Ram only on crossing 17) and re-run `sim.js`, default to the 15-space track for humans, and decide #6/#8. Before any public release: do #4.
